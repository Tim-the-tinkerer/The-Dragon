# Changelog

## 1.3.0

- Sample mode gives each channel its own dragon. The right channel walks the mirror of the phrase, so the sides stay apart.

## 1.2.0

- Grain mode can play a right turn backward. The switch is off until you turn it on. Restore uses the same phrase.

## 1.1.0

- Sample mode folds a full loaded song. Ten minutes of stereo is one segment per sample.
- A long sample fold can take about a minute.

## 1.0.0

- Fold audio on the Heighway dragon and export a WAV that still plays.
- Sample, frame, and grain. Automatic passes, or 1 through 7.
- A `dcaf` note after the audio records the fold. The phrase is not stored.
- Restore puts the samples back. The original file is left alone.
